### Ranked fixes (implemented)

1. **P1. Account was a dead end for owners.**
   - Before: "Adding properties, rooms and listings is the next part being built", with no link, while the owner portal already works.
   - After: a role-aware next step. Owners get "Open the owner portal" with their property and room counts; renters get "Browse rooms" and "Saved rooms". The page title "Your account" now uses the 32 px page-title scale.
   - Why: Nielsen #1, visibility of system status (the page misreported what exists).
2. **P1. Log-in copy promised things that don't exist.**
   - Before: "Your saved rooms and messages are waiting." There is no messaging, and saved rooms live on the device.
   - After: "Log in to your BoardEase account. Owners manage their properties and rooms from here."
   - Why: Nielsen #2, match between the system and the real world.
3. **P1. Listing-dialog close button was nearly invisible on light photos.**
   - After: a 40 px white disc with a shadow, matching the save button; the heart now sits 8 px clear of it.
   - Why: WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11, non-text contrast.
4. **P2. Empty owner dashboard used an icon in a tinted tile above a heading** (a template tell) and gave no sense of order.
   - After: a three-step checklist (property, rooms and beds, publish), with the current step in Fired Clay and its action inline.
   - Why: Nielsen #6, recognition rather than recall.
5. **P2. Half the owner nav was "Soon" items, and phone tabs were cut mid-word ("Room…").**
   - After: built modules first, then one "Coming later" group on desktop with no badges. Phones show only working tabs, with short labels (Dashboard / Properties / Rooms) that fit at 390 px.
   - Why: Nielsen #8, aesthetic and minimalist design.
6. **P2. The empty properties card stretched to the form's height.**
   - After: `items-start`, so the card keeps its own height.
   - Why: Nielsen #8.
